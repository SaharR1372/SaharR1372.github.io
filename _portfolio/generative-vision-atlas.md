[**Open the atlas**](https://saharr1372.github.io/genai-atlas/) · [Code](https://github.com/SaharR1372/genai-atlas)

A living literature review of generative vision, organized around one question:
**where should generation happen** — in a VAE latent, a foundation model's
features, discrete tokens, or raw pixels?

![Generative Vision Atlas](/images/genai-atlas.png)

- **204 papers** in **35 research lines**, each defined by the bet it makes
- Benchmark numbers always shown with the settings that produced them — no leaderboard
- Medical imaging covered as a separate domain
- Updated every three days from arXiv; every entry verified against its source
